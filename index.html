SIT223 - Professional Practice in Information Technology

Welcome to the official unit page for SIT223. This unit focuses on developing essential professional competencies, industry standards, agile project management methodologies (such as Scrum and Trello sprint planning), and collaborative version control workflows using Git and GitHub.

Unit Overview & Details:

  • Credit Points: 1 Credit Point
  • Enrolment Modes: Trimester 1 (Burwood, Waurn Ponds, Online), Trimester 2 (Burwood, Online)
  • Prerequisites: Must have completed a minimum of two SIT coded units.
  • Corequisites: STP010
  • Incompatible Units: SIT301, SIT753
  • Study Commitment: Approximately 150 hours over the teaching period.
  • Learning Structure: Campus learning (1 x 3-hour seminar per week) and online learning (independent and collaborative learning including 1 x 2-hour online seminar per week).

Key Learning Resources:

  • Access the official unit guide and announcements on CloudDeakin.
  • Review version control documentation via GitHub Guides.
  • Explore agile frameworks and board management through Trello.
