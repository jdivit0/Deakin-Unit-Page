SIT223 - Professional Practice in Information Technology

Welcome to the official unit page for SIT223. This unit focuses on professional practice in IT, requiring 1 Credit Point with a study commitment of approximately 150 hours over the teaching period.

Unit Overview & Details:

  • Enrolment Modes: Trimester 1 (Burwood, Waurn Ponds, Online), Trimester 2 (Burwood, Online)
  • Prerequisites: Must have completed a minimum of two SIT coded units.
  • Corequisites: STP010
  • Incompatible Units: SIT301, SIT753
  • Learning Structure: Campus learning includes 1 x 3-hour seminar per week, while online learning includes independent and collaborative learning with 1 x 2-hour online seminar per week.
